Experience

Current roleSenior Sales ManagerCloudflare · Amsterdam

I moved into sales management in January 2026 with two account executives and three open roles. We hired new people and merged with other teams. We’re closing out the year as a close-knit group of 13.

I set the growth strategy for Cloudflare’s Digital Native business in our markets across Europe and Central Asia. I decide where we focus our time, from getting to know promising companies early to finding new ways to help existing customers.

My coaching focuses on sales maturity, technical acumen and curiosity. I want each account executive to make their own call on a deal, understand how the customer’s product works and ask a better question when the first answer leaves something unclear.

As an AE, I could only speak to so many customers in a week. The biggest surprise in management has been how much I enjoy seeing someone take an idea that worked for me, make it their own and use it with a customer I’ll never meet. The best part is watching them succeed and thinking I might have helped a little.

Sep 2022 – Jan 2026Senior Territory Account ExecutiveCloudflare · Amsterdam

I spent just over three years as an account executive at Cloudflare, developing new business across Central Asia, the Caucasus and Southern Europe. I was promoted to Senior Territory Account Executive in July 2024.

I made President’s Club three years in a row (2023–2025) and won the Million Dollar Award and the Top MM AE Award (Q2 2024).

Cisco gave me a solid foundation in sales. Cloudflare taught me the rest. My background in IT infrastructure, networking and security shapes how I sell. I’m as comfortable discussing security vulnerabilities with an engineer as I am talking to a CEO about how AI could transform their business.

By then, I felt I’d figured out how to sell. I moved into management because I wanted to help other people find their own way of doing it.

Aug 2018 – Sep 2022Account ManagerCisco · Moscow

I managed Cisco’s relationships with the largest of Russia’s metals groups. In my first two years as an account manager, I developed new business and grew existing accounts across the Volga region, southern Russia and the Urals.

Jul 2017 – Aug 2018Associate Sales RepresentativeCisco · Netherlands

I joined the Cisco Sales Associates Program in the Netherlands, where I studied networking and worked alongside engineers and sales colleagues.

That year was a turning point in my life. I met some of my closest friends there, along with people who became my mentors. They opened my eyes to possibilities I’d never considered.
