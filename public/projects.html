Language learning

AI Wordcards

I learned English in three months using Yagodkin’s method, just before I started a master’s programme taught in English. I’d always wanted an app that would let me learn new words that way, so I built AI Wordcards.

The aim is to learn up to 100 foreign words in an hour. For each new word, find a word in your own language that sounds like it. Then picture the two meanings together in a single scene.

Take the English word ship, or корабль in Russian. It sounds like the Russian word шип, which means “thorn”. Imagine a ship sailing past with a giant rose thorn for a mast. The thorn gives you the sound, and the ship gives you the meaning.

A scene like that sticks in my head much longer than a pair of words on a page. The hard part was coming up with a fresh association for every word. That’s what the AI helps me do. If you speak Russian, give it a try.
